      How it runs

      1. Around 07:15 PKT every day GitHub Actions starts the pipeline. Nobody has to press anything.
      2. Reconcile the payment feed (JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank, payroll) against the loan book in SQL: duplicates, unknown references, overpaid loans.
      3. Age the book: SQL applies payments oldest-instalment-first and works out days past due for every loan.
      4. Watch employers: compares each partner's late-payment rate with its own normal level.
      5. Predict which upcoming instalments will be paid late (Python, scikit-learn).
      6. Plan the day: 30 calls per agent, with slots reserved for prevention; SMS for borrowers slightly late or at medium risk.
      7. Publish this page, the CSV call lists and the brief.
